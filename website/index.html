Core Capabilities

Built for Professionals Who Meet to Get Things Done

Engineered as a lightweight, privacy-first companion that sits directly inside your browser without running invasive meeting bot accounts.

🎙️

Unlimited Meeting Recording

Captures every spoken line via browser live captions. Employs a chunked, streaming IndexedDB pipeline with zero audio/video memory bloat, allowing marathon 4+ hour calls without slowing down your computer.

  • No artificial 1-hour recording caps
  • No bot invitations required in calls
  • Zero memory leaks with streaming buffer
📋

Automated Minutes of Meeting (MOM)

Converts entire meetings into pristine, structured Minutes of Meeting within seconds. Formatted as professional Markdown or clean Plaintext for instant sharing in Slack, Notion, or Email.

  • Executive Summary & Attendee Roster
  • Numbered list of settled agreements
  • Action item tables with owners & deadlines
⚡

Real-Time Déjà Vu Detection

Prevents teams from wasting time re-debating topics settled weeks ago. Powered by a 512-dimension local feature-hashing vectorizer that flags duplicate decisions live as you speak.

  • Instant in-call alert with prior meeting citation
  • Attendee overlap & date context
  • Fully customizable similarity sensitivity
☁️

Google Drive Cloud Sync

Automatically backs up structured MOM documents and full transcripts to a private "Precedent Meeting Notes" folder in your Google Drive using the least-privilege drive.file scope.

  • Zero access to your other Google Drive files
  • Auto-uploads when meeting tab wraps up
  • Direct web links to view saved documents
🔐

Supabase Authentication & Sync

Connect your Supabase project with zero dependencies. Securely manage user sessions, cross-device commitment syncing, and password or magic link authentication directly in Manifest V3.

  • Pre-configured with public anon key support
  • Automatic session refresh in service worker
  • 100% offline fallback when disconnected
🛡️

100% Local-First Privacy

Your audio and video never leave your machine. Precedent processes live caption text locally in browser memory. No third-party LLM providers sniffing your meeting conversations.

  • Zero analytics trackers or advertising IDs
  • Granular data retention (30 to 365 days or forever)
  • One-click JSON export & instant wipe button

Quick Setup

Install in 60 Seconds

Precedent runs as a Manifest V3 developer extension in Google Chrome, Brave, Arc, or Microsoft Edge.

01

Download & Extract ZIP

Download the pre-packaged release ZIP archive and extract it to any convenient folder on your computer (e.g. Downloads/precedent-extension).

Download precedent-extension.zip
02

Open Chrome Extensions

Open a new tab in Chrome, paste the following URL into your address bar, and press Enter:

chrome://extensions

Toggle on "Developer mode" in the top-right corner.

03

Load Unpacked Extension

Click the "Load unpacked" button in the top-left corner and select the extracted precedent-extension folder.

✓ Precedent icon will appear in your extensions bar
04

Join a Call & Turn on Captions

Open Google Meet, Zoom Web, or Microsoft Teams. Join any meeting and enable Live Captions [CC]. Precedent activates automatically!

• Floating overlay appears with real-time memory
Privacy Architecture

Your Meetings Belong to You

Built according to strict zero-telemetry and local-first principles. Transparent, verifiable, and open source.

🔒

Local-Only In-Browser Processing

Precedent reads live caption text generated by your browser. No audio or video streams are ever recorded, encoded, or transmitted to any external server.

📂

Restricted Google Drive Permission

When you connect Google Drive, Precedent requests only the narrow drive.file scope. It can only read and write files created by itself in the "Precedent Meeting Notes" folder, with zero access to your existing files or folders.

🚫

No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers

Precedent contains zero Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, advertising SDKs, or behavioral telemetry. Your IndexedDB database resides solely on your computer.

🗑️

Full User Control & Deletion

You can set automatic pruning from 30 to 365 days, export your entire meeting history in a single JSON file, or permanently delete all data with one click in extension settings.
